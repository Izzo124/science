Chapter 1: Stimuli and Responses (Human Vision)

Light rays enter through the cornea, pass through the pupil (controlled by the iris), and are refracted by the biconvex lens to form an inverted, sharp image on the Yellow Spot (Fovea centralis), which has the highest density of photoreceptors (cones and rods). Ciliary muscles change the curvature of the eye lens to focus near or distant objects.

Chapters 2 & 5: Respiration & Thermochemistry

Exothermic reactions release heat to the surroundings (ΔH < 0), raising temperature (combustion, runaway thermal cores). Endothermic reactions absorb heat energy from surroundings (ΔH > 0), dropping temperature (dissolving Ammonium Nitrate, NH4NO3, in cold packs). Respiration requires continuous supply of O2 and removal of CO2 through alveoli.

Chapter 3: Transportation in Plants & Chemical Synthesis

Water synthesized via 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O is absorbed by plant roots and transported upwards through xylem vessels via transpiration pull, capillary action, and root pressure. Excess water evaporates through leaf stomata, regulated by guard cells.

Chapter 4: Reactivity Series of Metals & Extraction

Metals less reactive than Carbon (such as Iron, Zinc, Lead) are extracted from their ores in a Blast Furnace via reduction by Carbon (Coke) and Carbon Monoxide: 2Fe2O3 + 3C → 4Fe + 3CO2↑. High reactivity metals (K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al) must be extracted by electrolysis.

Chapter 6: Electricity and Magnetism

Transmission heat loss is given by Ploss = I2R. Stepping up voltage via a Step-Up Transformer (Vp/Vs = Np/Ns, where Ns > Np) to 132 kV reduces current (I), minimizing heat dissipation. At residential zones, a Step-Down Transformer (Ns < Np) drops voltage to safe 240 V.

Chapter 7: Energy, Work and Power

Work Done (W) = Force (F) × Displacement (s) in Joules (J). Gravitational Potential Energy: Ep = mgh (mass × gravity 9.8 m/s2 × height). Kinetic Energy: Ek = ½mv2.

Chapter 8: Radioactivity & Shielding

Alpha (α): High ionizing power, low penetration, stopped by a single sheet of paper. Beta (β): Moderate penetration, stopped by 5mm aluminium sheet. Gamma (γ): High energy electromagnetic waves, stopped only by thick dense lead (Pb) or concrete.

Chapters 9 & 10: Space Weather & Space Exploration

Solar flares and Coronal Mass Ejections (CME) blast streams of charged plasma that trigger geomagnetic storms, disrupting communications and power grids. Planetary geomagnetic shields deflect solar wind. Satellite orbits obey Kepler's laws in elliptical paths.
